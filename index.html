Grid Tab — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Grid Tab is a Chrome browser extension that replaces your new tab page with a customisable dashboard. Grid Tab has no backend server of its own, no analytics, and no tracking. Your content stays in your browser. The only exceptions — Chrome account sync and Google's public favicon service — are described below.

Data We Store

Everything below is created by you and stored in your browser via chrome.storage.local and localStorage:

  • Your name and appearance settings — theme, accent colour, background, borders, and per-widget colours.
  • Widget layout — which widgets you use, their positions and sizes.
  • Bookmarks & folders you add — name, URL and any icon.
  • Tasks, calendar reminders, and notes.
  • An image you add to the Image widget — stored locally as a data URL.

Chrome Account Sync

So your setup follows you across devices, Grid Tab also saves your settings (excluding large images) to chrome.storage.sync. This data is stored and synced by Google as part of your own Chrome/Google account and is governed by Google's privacy policy. Grid Tab itself never receives this data on any server — it only reads and writes it within your signed-in browser. If Chrome Sync is off, no sync occurs.

Permissions

  • storage — Saves the data listed above on your device and, via Chrome sync, to your own Google account.
  • bookmarks — Read-only. Used only for the optional "Import from Chrome" feature, which copies your existing bookmarks into the dashboard. Your bookmarks are never modified or transmitted anywhere.

Third-Party Requests

  • Google Favicons — When a bookmark uses an automatic icon, the site's favicon is fetched from www.google.com/s2/favicons. The only information sent is the website's domain name.
  • Web search — If you type a query in the command bar and press Enter with no match, Grid Tab opens a Google search for that text. Nothing is sent until you press Enter.

These are the only outbound network requests Grid Tab makes.

Data Sharing

Grid Tab does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. There is no Grid Tab server, no advertising, and no third-party analytics or tracking of any kind.

Children's Privacy

Grid Tab does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.
